ttkConverter invokes separately packaged command-line tools.

FFmpeg and ffprobe: https://ffmpeg.org/ (see ffmpeg -version and -buildconf).
The GPL build includes x264, x265, libaom, libvpx, Opus, LAME, Vorbis, WebP and zlib.
Open-source license texts and corresponding source information accompany the release.
LLVM MinGW runtime components: https://github.com/mstorsjo/llvm-mingw/releases/tag/20261117
Winpthreads: https://github.com/mingw-w64/mingw-w64/tree/41eb042358a52011b35350392130dcdc94a8ead8/mingw-w64-libraries/winpthreads
AMD AMF SDK headers: https://github.com/GPUOpen-LibrariesAndSDKs/AMF/tree/v1.4.35
NVIDIA FFmpeg codec headers: https://github.com/FFmpeg/nv-codec-headers/tree/n12.2.72.0

Xpdf Tools 4.06: https://www.xpdfreader.com/download.html
Corresponding source: https://dl.xpdfreader.com/xpdf-4.06.tar.gz
Chinese character maps: https://dl.xpdfreader.com/xpdf-chinese-simplified.tar.gz

Tesseract 5.3.0: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract/tree/5.3.0
Leptonica 1.83.1: https://github.com/DanBloomberg/leptonica/tree/1.83.1
PNG 1.6.59: https://github.com/pnggroup/libpng/tree/v1.6.59
FreeType 2.14.3: https://download.savannah.gnu.org/releases/freetype/
English and Chinese fast OCR models: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast/tree/4.1.0

Inno Setup installation creator: https://jrsoftware.org/isinfo.php
